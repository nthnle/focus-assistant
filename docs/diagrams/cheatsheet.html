Using Focus Assistant
Commands go in a terminal on the Pi (ssh in). Blue is what you can say to the coach instead, by Siri or “Hey Jarvis”.

Every workday

BEFORE 9Up to three priorities: focus add … “add email Sam”. Phone on its charger.
09:00Morning check-in. Start with what it names.
FOCUS TIMEPhone stays on the charger. Stand up at :50. Done? Press the Call button, or “done with the report”.
A MEETING“Quiet until 11”, or focus pause 11:00. Check-ins wait; reminders skip.
13:00Midday check-in. Drifted? Take the one reset it gives you.
17:30Evening check-in. Add tomorrow’s first thing now. Phone away for the night.

Every week

MONDAYSet one to three goals: focus goal add … “this week I want to ship the beta”. Set on the weekend, they count for the week ahead.
NOT TODAYfocus add --on wed … or focus later 3. focus now 3 brings it back.
DAY OFF“Off until Monday”, or focus pause monday. Nothing counts as slipping.
FRIDAY 17:30Wrap-up: goals, what slipped, and one routine tip. Try it next week. Ask any time: “how could I improve my routine?”

Now and then

A REPLY MISSESfocus rate bad "why" “that was wrong, it’s Sunday”. A good one: focus rate good.
EVERY 2 WEEKSfocus feedback, then bring it to a Claude Code session to improve the coach.
MONTHLYUpdate the Pi (Updating in docs/install.md). Check the copies in ~/focus-backups on the PC.
SOMETHING’S OFFfocus status, then Troubleshooting in docs/install.md.
